# Material Provenance
The textures here are original photography-based PBR assets from Poly Haven under CC0.
- **gantry_metal**: [metal_plate](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate) (CC0)
- **casino_marble**: [marble_tiles](https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_tiles) (CC0)
- **weathered_hull**: [metal_plate_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02) (CC0)

The renderer can fall back to original materials if scanned texture files fail to load.
